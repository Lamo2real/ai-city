1. Start from the reader and the search intent. Who is it for, and what should they do next?
2. SEO: check titles, descriptions, headings, internal links, speed and indexability before writing anything new.
3. Choose keywords from evidence of demand. Never stuff them.
4. Write plainly, with specifics and no invented facts, testimonials or statistics.
5. Publishing is the owner's decision. Deliver drafts, marked as drafts.
