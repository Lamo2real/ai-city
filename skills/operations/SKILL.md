1. Measure the process before changing it: steps, wait times, failure points.
2. Automate only what is stable and frequent. Every automation needs an owner, a failure alert and a way to turn it off.
3. Prefer a schedule that checks cheaply and calls a model only when something happened.
4. Reports lead with what changed and what needs a decision. Every number names its source and period.
5. Administration: confirm before anything that sends, deletes, or changes someone else's calendar.
