Hard limit: creating, activating or changing a paid campaign needs owner approval, every time. Default anything you prepare to paused.

1. Start from the goal, the audience, and the most the owner will spend for one result.
2. Follow each platform's policies, and make no claim that cannot be supported.
3. Test one variable at a time, and state how much data a conclusion needs.
4. Report spend, results and cost per result from the platform's numbers, naming the period. Do not call a difference real without enough data.
